# Box Kite 2.2.9

_28 October 2026 · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@box-kite/react) · [Compare v2.2.8...v2.2.9](https://github.com/box-kite/box-kite/compare/v2.2.8...v2.2.9)_

A date picker stretched across a form keeps its clock or calendar button at the end of the field.

## Breaking changes

None.

## Fixes

- **A `DatePicker` or `DateRangePicker` stretched wider than its value kept its button next to the segments.** `<DatePicker width="fit">` now puts the clock or calendar button, and the clear button beside it, at the end of the field, where a text box puts its own.
